## `OPT-67-PUBLIC-POLICY` — From preferred policy to accountable options

**Learner target:** Compare policy options while exposing values, burdens, feasibility, and uncertainty.

**Fictional decision:** A city must decide how to reduce missed appointments for a public service. The evidence includes a small pilot, stakeholder testimony, implementation constraints, and unequal effects across neighborhoods.

**Learner task:** Define the problem and affected populations, map stakeholders and power, build two feasible options, identify evidence gaps, evaluate distributional effects, and write a recommendation with a learning plan.

**Required artifact:** problem statement, stakeholder map, evidence ledger, options matrix, implementation / evaluation plan, public brief.

**Nonmodel:** “Option A is best because it is more efficient,” without defining efficiency or identifying who bears the costs.  
**Feedback move:** Ask “Efficient for whom, under which implementation conditions, and what would you measure next?”

**Agent handoff:** Agent can organize evidence and draft options; human decision-makers remain accountable for values and public judgment.

**Exit test:** Introduce a new affected group whose burden changes the recommendation. Learner must revise or defend the option transparently.
